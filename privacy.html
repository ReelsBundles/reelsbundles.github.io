Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to ReelsBundles. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. If you have any questions or concerns about our policy, or our practices with regards to your personal info, please contact us at support@reelsbundles.com.

When you visit our website and use our services, you trust us with your personal information. We take your privacy very seriously. In this privacy notice, we describe our privacy policy. We seek to explain to you in the clearest way possible what info we collect, how we use it, and what rights you have in relation to it.

2. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when registering at our website, purchasing our products, or contacting support.

The personal information that we collect depends on the context of your interactions with us, and may include:

  • Personal Details: Full Name, email address, phone number.
  • Account Credentials: Secure authentication tokens linked to your Google/Firebase Login.
  • Payment Information: Transaction details (handled securely by our checkout partner Cashfree). We do not store credit/debit card numbers directly on our servers.
  • Access Metrics: IP addresses and User-Agent fingerprints to verify security entitlements and prevent account sharing.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information collected via our website for a variety of business purposes described below:

  • To facilitate account creation and logon processes via Firebase.
  • To fulfill and manage your product purchases, order verification, and processing.
  • To deliver dynamic download files securely via Google Drive streams without exposing direct links.
  • To protect our content library and monitor concurrent device logins to prevent malicious sharing.
  • To send support updates and transactional ticketing information.

4. Information Sharing & Third Parties

We only share information with your consent, to comply with laws, or to provide you with services. We work with the following trusted third-party integrations:

  • Google Firebase: User login, credential verification, and account profile storage.
  • Cashfree Payments: Encrypted payment checkout integration and webhook order processing.
  • Google Drive APIs: Safe streaming and folder mapping of Reels packages.

5. Data Security

We implement a variety of security measures to maintain the safety of your personal information when you place an order or access your downloads. All connection links utilize SSL/TLS encryption. However, please remember that no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions or comments about this policy, you may email us at reelsbundles.support@gmail.com or submit a ticket through our Contact Page.
